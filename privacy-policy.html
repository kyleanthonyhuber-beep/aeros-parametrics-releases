Aeros Parametrics — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 11, 2026 · App: Aeros Parametrics (package com.aerosparametrics.app) · Developer: Kyle Huber

Summary

Aeros Parametrics is a fully offline, on-device CAD app. It has no network access at all — the app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send or receive anything over the network. The developer collects no data, and no data is shared with anyone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no telemetry and no AI or cloud features.

Your files

Parts, assemblies and drawings are stored only on your device — in the app's private storage or a location you choose with the Android file picker. They leave the device only when you explicitly use Share… or save them to a location you pick (for example a cloud folder offered by the system picker); the app itself never uploads anything.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions: no Internet, no camera, no microphone, no storage, no location and no contacts. Opening and saving files uses the Android system file picker, which grants access only to the file you choose.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting data

All data is on your device: delete files you saved, or uninstall the app to remove its private storage.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published with app updates and on this page.
